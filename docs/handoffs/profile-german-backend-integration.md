## Integrated behavior and review fixes

- Rich caregiver create/edit, optional private portrait/pictures and context retrieval. Six new exact demo bridge operations remain private-gate/same-origin/demo-only and blocked by the participant conversation lock; image admission now shares the existing bounded upload guard. Pixels never reach the chat model. Captions/context are caregiver-provided facts, not inferred identity.
- Separate summary-memory permission defaults off. This explicitly permitted feature allows short paraphrased topic summaries while journal excerpts remain off; it does not authorize transcripts/audio or silently enable journal/preference saving. Existing profiles start off after migration006; no bulk fictional backfill or permission change is performed.
- The source awaited optional summarization before returning the ordinary reply. Changed it to one bounded background job per profile, canceled by new input, controls and revision changes, with a two-second deadline and the existing callback/checkpoint path. Speech does not wait for memory. Failed/rejected summaries remain absent; no fake success or retry. Completed summaries stay source-labeled unreviewed AI context, maximum300 characters/ten per profile; retrieval chooses up to three within the adapter's intact5,000-character prompt bound.
- Added mechanical summary rejection of full utterance echoes/copied stretches, quotations, contact payloads, unsupported action claims and bounded clinical/treatment markers. This supplements the prompt; it does not prove universal semantic correctness or secure erasure.
- Fixed delayed refusal checkpoints deleting newer authorized memory, and stale checked caregiver forms silently re-enabling memory after refusal. Refusal disables/deletes only at matching profile/privacy revisions and advances the profile revision when permission changes. A fresh refusal after a saved future-preset update binds the current authoritative revision while retaining the active presentation preset. Deletion and delayed summary writes remain fenced.
- German language comes from an exact server-side profile-ID allowlist, propagated through setup/ready/text and every microphone turn. Existing fictional Tilda was found and reused; her exact ID is stored privately. No Alex context, biography, permissions or endpoint credential is copied. Other profiles remain English unless explicitly mapped. German activities remain unavailable; German ordinary chat skips Jev activity classification, avoiding an unused provider request/failure dependency. English activity-only Jev stays enabled.
- Preserved caregiver preselection, one-action half-duplex browser listening, Quiet waiting, one-second ending, eight-second chat deadline, ASR aliases and grounded warmer family replies. Added German fabricated-contact rejection while preserving denials/practical support. No model/voice fallback or automatic listening after reconnect.
